# AWS Student Builder FAQs and Resources

## AWS Builder Center Student Rewards

### What are AWS Student Rewards?
AWS Student Rewards is a program that provides students with free access to AWS services, training, and resources.

### How do I claim Student Rewards?
1. Sign up on AWS Builder Center (https://builder.aws.com)
2. Verify your student status with your .edu email
3. Claim your Skill Builder Premium subscription
4. Access AWS Console to start building

### Do I need a .edu email to sign up on Builder Center?
No! You can sign up on AWS Builder Center without a .edu email initially. The .edu email is only required when verifying your student status in step 2.

### What's included in Student Rewards?
- AWS Skill Builder Premium access (normally $29/month)
- AWS credits for hands-on learning
- Access to AWS Builder Center community events
- Student-focused learning paths and certifications

### How long does student verification take?
Student verification typically takes 1-3 business days after you submit your .edu email.

## AWS Portals - What's the Difference?

### AWS Builder Center
- Community hub for learning and events
- Sign up first (no .edu required)
- Claim student rewards here
- Access: https://builder.aws.com

### AWS Skill Builder
- Training platform with courses and labs
- Premium access through Student Rewards
- Hands-on labs and certification prep
- Access: https://skillbuilder.aws

### AWS Console
- Where you actually build and deploy projects
- Requires AWS account creation
- Use your credits here to build real applications
- Access: https://console.aws.amazon.com

## Common Questions

### I signed up on Builder Center, now what?
Next step: Verify your student status by connecting your university .edu email address.

### Where do I claim my credits?
After student verification is complete, credits appear in the AWS Billing console under "Credits".

### Can I use AWS without a credit card?
For student accounts through AWS Educate, you can access many services without a credit card.

### My verification is pending, what should I do?
Verification takes 1-3 business days. Check your .edu email inbox for verification link.

### Which portal do I use to start building?
After completing all steps, use AWS Console (console.aws.amazon.com) to build projects.
